Third-party work used by this site
===================================

The site's scripts are bundled and minified, which strips the credit comments
their sources carry. The notices those licences ask to travel with the code are
kept here instead. The typefaces' licences are beside the font files, in
/assets/fonts/.


1. Simplex noise (GLSL)
-----------------------
The 3D simplex noise function in the Home page's contour field.
Author: Ian McEwan, Ashima Arts. Maintainer: Stefan Gustavson.
https://github.com/ashima/webgl-noise
https://github.com/stegu/webgl-noise

Copyright (C) 2011 by Ashima Arts (Simplex noise)
Copyright (C) 2011-2016 by Stefan Gustavson (Classic noise and others)

Distributed under the MIT License (text below).


2. fluid-three
--------------
The pointer fluid on the Home page is a port of the stable-fluids solver in
mnmxmx/fluid-three, https://github.com/mnmxmx/fluid-three, which its
repository offers under the MIT Licence (text below).


3. f1-circuits
--------------
The Madring and Sepang circuit outlines are derived from bacinger/f1-circuits,
https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits (circuits/es-2026.geojson and
circuits/my-1999.geojson).

Copyright (c) 2019-2025 Tomislav Bacinger

Used under the MIT License (text below).
